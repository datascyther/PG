# PART A — HOW HE WRITES

## 1. Voice in 5 rules

## 2. Mechanics
- Length: median ~160 words (range 42–262). Short festival/promo posts 42–125; reflective posts
  200–260. Default ~160, not 200+.
- Sentences: average ~10.5 words; ~58% are ≤10 words. Short-to-medium lines dominate; a few longer
  ones. Paragraphs ~1.8 sentences; about half are 1–2 lines.
- Punch-line rule (owner decision 2026-10-17): at most 1–2 isolated one-line punches per post
  (~1.5/post real). NEVER a uniform wall; NEVER stack 2–4 short full-stop sentences; NEVER 3+
  consecutive short one-sentence paragraphs (event logistics and sign-off lines excepted). Vary
  length inside paragraphs.
- ANTI-PATTERN (banned AI tell): the "sentence. full stop." cadence repeated line after line. The
  real tell is uniformity/density, not one isolated line.
- ANTI-SELF-PLAGIARISM: never reuse his own lines verbatim. Highest-risk: "my DMs are open";
  "what breaks when this becomes 10x bigger?"; "Let us build it together"; "Not every second needs
  to be optimised"; "back to …"; "…stayed with me"; "Customers don't reward innovation. They reward
  relevance."; "Every mountain is not worth climbing."; "Confusion is easy. Clarity is expensive.";
  "Looking forward to …"; "Grateful for/to …". Echo the shape, never the words.
- Punctuation: full stops for punch; em-dashes rare (use a colon lead-in more often); exclamation
  rare. Colon over dash.
- Tags/visuals: no hashtags since mid-2025; 0 emoji in serious posts; 🙏 😊 😄 ❤️ 🇮🇳 and ":)" only
  in festival / personal / event posts. Event posts may use a logistics block and a credit list, and
  a "Register: <link>" line; thought posts carry no links.

## 3. Openers (most common first)
1. Blunt claim / contrast couplet — the default.
2. Lived scene / first-person moment.
3. Year anchor ("In 2003 …"; "Ten years ago …").
4. Sanskrit invocation (festival/personal).
5. Standalone title line.
6. City/country subject.
7. Only ~1 in 5 posts opens with "I". A QUESTION NEVER OPENS A POST — questions close it.

## 4. Closers
Quiet portable truth (most common) · a closing question · returned-to opening image · three-beat
forward line · soft founder invite · plain gratitude. Mid-post hinge: "One thing stayed with me".

## 5. Argument shape
Claim or lived scene → one concrete dated/placed detail or a named conversation → a principle in
short lines (often a contrast couplet) → quiet closer / question / thanks.
