## 0. How to use this file

**Moment tiers**
| Tier | Meaning |
|---|---|
| M | Archived moment from a real published post — the moment is public; the *wording* is his and must not be reused |
| L | LIVE moment: mentioned in one passing line only, never expanded. Expandable into a full post **without repeating a word he wrote** |
| N | New moment, harvested from the owner (daily capture — see `16_RAW_BANK.md`) |

**Receipt tiers**
| Tier | Meaning |
|---|---|
| A | Stated by the owner himself, publicly (own post, own site, own profile) |
| B | Third-party press or official publication (citable with attribution) |
| C | Profile/award/patent record (`pg.pdf`, official award lists) |
| D | Inference. **Never publishable.** Quarantined. |

**Publish flags** (every row carries one)
| Flag | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `OK` | Publishable as-is |
| `PRESS-ATTRIB` | Publishable only with the source named ("per Business Standard, July 2025") |
| `ASK` | Needs the owner's clearance — a third party's name is involved |
| `PAUSED` | 2008–2016 chapter. **Never publish.** Internal reference only |
| `NDA` | Do not publish under any circumstances |

**Three hard rules**
1. If a figure, name, place or scene is not in this file with a source, it does not go in a draft.
2. `PAUSED` rows are never published, and no defence/military vocabulary or named military
   location may be written in any post (see `21_COMPLIANCE.md`).
3. The current venture is referenced only as "current stealth AI venture" or "building sovereign
   AI infrastructure". No product, partner, customer, investor, competitor or fund name.

## 7. What the data says about anchors (do not over-claim)

Of the 50 top posts: 25 name a place or institution, 25 name a third party, 22 use a number as
evidence, 7 carry a hard calendar date, 17 carry a relative-time anchor ("a year ago", "last
week"). Posts with any such anchor show a median ER of 8.10% against 1.93% for those without.

**That comparison cannot be used as a causal claim.** 8 of the 11 no-anchor posts are from the
pre-2026-07-18 regime (account-wide ER was 1.01%), 17 of the 50 were coded from URL slugs only
(all pre-July), and the full-text control group is 2 posts — one of which scores 11.12%. Inside
the post-July window the gap narrows to 11.30% vs 8.21% on n=3.

**What is safe to say:** anchors are what his best work does, and nothing in the data suggests they
hurt. Anchors are a voice device first and a performance device second. Judge posts on ER, never on
impressions.

## 8. The shape that wins — read from the recovered bodies

With all 50 bodies now readable, the design lesson is no longer a guess. Three shapes carry the
account, and one is a trap.

**Shape A — the institution post (the best post of the period: 3,048 impressions, 402 engagements,
13.19% ER, 2026-07-22).** Institution + year + named people + a single quotable line:
> "Indian Institute of Technology, Delhi has always remained close to my entrepreneurial journey…
> Founders build companies. Institutions build generations of founders."

**Shape B — the room post (5,580 impressions, 283 engagements, 5.07% ER, 2026-07-19).** A real
place he was in, on a real day, doing something ordinary, and the reflection lands on the reader:
a public library on a Sunday, a book read cover to cover, no notifications.

**Shape C — the scene that carries a lesson (6,010 impressions, 1,155 engagements, 19.22% ER,
2026-09-14).** A person in a room, what they said, and what an operator takes from it. The
highest-engagement post of the entire year is a conversation, not a thesis.

**The trap — the national-impact post (74,295 impressions, 159 engagements, 0.21% ER,
2025-12-06).** Clean, well-argued, numbers-heavy, and nobody cared. The largest reach of the year
is the smallest engagement of the year. **Do not copy that shape. Do not chase that subject.**

**The rule these four posts write for us:** the winning posts are small in reach, specific in
material and generous to the reader. The losing post is broad, clever and about something
abstract. A post needs a **room**, a **moment** and a **person** — not a **topic**.
