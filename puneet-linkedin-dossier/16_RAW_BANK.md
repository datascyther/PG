# 16_RAW_BANK.md — Live Raw-Material Bank

> **INTERNAL ONLY — DO NOT SHARE.** Contains the owner's own words captured from WhatsApp
> voice notes and messages. Never publish his raw words verbatim without the derivation rules in
> `17_DRAFTS/TEMPLATE.md`. Not for external distribution.

**Created:** 2026-10-21
**Purpose:** the inbox where every piece of raw material lands before anything is drafted. One
sentence from the owner is enough. This file makes that sentence usable, and it is where the
anchor test is applied so vague material never reaches a draft.

**The standing ask (26 words, sent daily, WhatsApp, 07:45 IST):**
> Send me one thing from today — what happened, where, and what it made you think. Voice note is
> fine. "Nothing today" is also fine.

"Nothing today" is a valid, zero-cost answer. A prompt you can fail is a prompt you stop answering.

---

## 1. Rules

1. **His words are logged verbatim.** Never paraphrase into this file. Paraphrase is where the
   ghostwriter enters the system.
2. **Anchor test at ingest** (within 4 hours): the item must contain at least one of
   {place, date/year, number, named person, object, named decision}.
   - **Pass** → goes to the drafting queue.
   - **Fail** → shelved as a **thesis seed**. A thesis seed is never drafted on its own. It may
     only be used as the closing argument of a post whose body comes from a scene, and it must be
     re-anchored first. If a seed has no scene attached by Sunday, it is dropped.
3. **Subject date** is recorded at ingest — the date the thing actually happened, not the date it
   was captured. Subject age is computed from it, and it decides whether the item can still be
   published (see `20_CADENCE.md`, 7-day rule).
4. **One material item → at most 2 posts**, and only if the second uses a different pillar *and* a
   different idea.
5. **Bank floor: 10 usable items.** Below 5, the weekly review must act (seed evergreen posts from
   `15_REAL_WORLD_ANCHORS.md` §2 and shift a slot to a warm scene or a festival).
6. **One question per draft maximum**, factual, answerable in under 15 seconds ("40 or 400?",
   "who was in the room?"). Deadline 12 hours, then the answerless clause is dropped or the post is
   killed. Never ask him what he thinks about a draft.
