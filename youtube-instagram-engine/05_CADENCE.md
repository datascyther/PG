# 05 — CADENCE AND THE BATCH

One calendar, three surfaces. The LinkedIn engine already runs a fortnightly batch approved
in one 20-minute meeting; YouTube and Instagram join that meeting rather than getting their
own. If this creates a second approval loop, it will not survive month two.

---

## 1. The week

| Day | Time (IST) | YouTube | Instagram | LinkedIn |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Monday | 08:30 | — | 1 card | 1 post (Family P) |
| Wednesday | 19:00 | 1 long-form | 1 reel (the best cut) | 1 post (Family N) |
| Friday | 08:30 | 2 Shorts | 1 carousel | 1 post (Family P) |
| Saturday | 11:00 | 1 Short | 2 story frames | — |
| Sunday | — | rest | rest | — |

- **19:00 Wednesday for long-form is a house default, not a finding.** There is no watch-time
  data for this channel yet. Do not claim otherwise; revisit after eight episodes.
- Sunday is genuinely empty. A channel that posts seven days a week cannot be reviewed by one
  person.
- The Saturday 11:00 Short exists because the long-form is two days old by then and the
  Short's job is to reach people who never saw it.

---

## 2. The fortnight

Two weeks is the unit, because that is what fits in one meeting.

| Slot | What | Count per fortnight |
|---|---|---|
| Long-form | 1 On the Floor / Teardown / Post-Mortem / Versus / Gap / 2035 | 2 |
| Long Conversation | guest episode | 1 (monthly, not fortnightly) |
| Shorts | 3 per long-form | 6 |
| Reels | 1 per long-form | 2 |
| Carousels | re-cut from a long-form | 2 |
| Cards | one line, one image | 2 |
| Story frames | shoot days only | ~10 |

That is 6 feed posts on Instagram, 2 long-forms and 6 Shorts on YouTube, plus the 6 LinkedIn
posts already in flight. All of it comes from **two moments**, not from fourteen ideas. That
is the only reason it is achievable.

---

## 3. One moment, eight surfaces

```
MOMENT (dated scene + receipt, anchor bank)
  │
  ├── long-form master ............ the 8-12 min episode
  │     ├── Short: the number
  │     ├── Short: the admission
  │     ├── Short: the objection
  │     └── Reel: the number, vertical, re-cut
  ├── carousel ................... the argument in six slides
  ├── card ....................... the one line you would screenshot
  ├── LinkedIn post .............. already written by the other engine
  └── story frames ............... three, on the day of the shoot
```

The **card line is written first**, before the shoot. If the line is not strong enough to
carry a card, the episode is not strong enough to shoot. This is the cheapest possible kill
switch: ten minutes of writing saves a shoot day.

---

## 4. The drop order

Something will go wrong every fortnight. Decide now what is sacrificed, in this order:

1. Story frames
2. The card
3. The carousel
4. The third Short
5. The reel
6. **Never** the long-form — an empty Wednesday is a visible failure; a thin carousel is not
7. **Never** the approval step

---

## 5. Approval — inherited, unchanged

- **Explicit yes per asset.** A batch is not a blanket approval. Silence means held.
- **Batched.** One 20-minute meeting per fortnight. His side of it is three replies.
- **One word kills an asset.** No explanation, no penalty, no follow-up.
- **Nothing publishes from this folder.** Scripts and captions are rendered for a human;
  uploads happen by hand.
- **Two items need his answer before they can be scheduled:** Chapter D
  (`07_GOVERNANCE.md` §1) and the guest list (`02_DOMAIN_MAP.md` §8).

Approval message format, reuse from the LinkedIn dossier's WhatsApp templates:

> "Three videos, seven posts. Reply with the numbers you want, or 'none'. 1 = warehouse
> Sunday, 2 = 20 ml teardown, 3 = packaging post-mortem."

---

## 6. Capacity — the honest version

| Task | Hours per fortnight |
|---|---|
| Scripting 2 long-forms | 6 |
| Shoot day (2 locations) | 8 |
| Edit, two long-forms | 14 |
| Shorts and reels (6 cuts) | 6 |
| Carousels and cards (4 assets) | 5 |
| Captions, titles, thumbnails | 3 |
| Approval meeting | 0.5 |
| **Total** | **~42** |

Forty-two hours is roughly one full-time person, or two people at half their week. The deck
does not scope this. If nobody owns those hours, the realistic output is **one long-form a
month**, not one a week — and the honest move is to plan for that now rather than miss
eight Wednesdays in a row.

Recommended minimum viable slate if capacity is one person part-time:

| Frequency | Format | Why that one |
|---|---|---|
| Every 2 weeks | On the Floor or Teardown | the formats that prove the premise |
| Weekly | 1 Short | the only reach surface |
| Weekly | 1 card | the cheapest asset, five minutes of work |
| Monthly | The Long Conversation | needs a guest pipeline, so it cannot be weekly at first |
