## 4. Brainstorm: what makes this channel different

1. **The decision lens.** Wars, budgets, pitches and hires are all decisions made under uncertainty.
   Defence, investing and founding share one skill, and he has done all three. That is the through-line
   that lets one person speak across five domains without it looking scattered.
2. **The audit, not the celebration.** Most defence content celebrates. Defence Gaps, War Breakdown and
   the Investor's Table all take a critical view. Critic is a rarer and more credible position.
3. **Both sides of the table.** He raised capital and sat on a jury; he built for the forces and
   studies the technology. Few creators have stood on both sides of any of these.
4. **Defence as the door, not the room.** Defence draws the first view; the other four pillars
   keep a different audience subscribed.
5. **Plain language on hard systems.** Weapon of the Week, AI in 60 Seconds and Startup Breakdown are
   the same promise: a complex system in a few minutes.

## 6b. Deck flow, strategic theme and the growth logic

Both decks run: Content Strategy → Index → Ideas → What the ideas are → Strategic theme → Why
(growth and engagement). Each pillar slide reads idea → example ideas → strategic theme → why it grows.

| Pillar | Strategic theme | Why it drives growth and engagement |
|---|---|---|
| The Strategic Affairs | The decision behind the event | Topical, so the main discovery engine; Versus and Weapon of the Week build a weekly habit |
| Startup Breakdown | One decision, one number | Saved and shared; carousels of numbers are the most saveable piece |
| The Investor's Table | Both sides of the table | Questions invite comments; founders tag co-founders |
| Journey Decoded | The real decision, with the number and the cost | People subscribe to a person; drives comments and profile visits |
| AI Decoded | Who owns it, what it costs, what it changes | Pulls a non-defence audience; evergreen search views |

Channel-wide theme (every piece): The Moment → The Context → The Insider View → The Takeaway.
Growth funnel and its measures: Discover (views, reach) → Watch (average view duration, retention) →
Engage (comments, saves, shares) → Follow (subscribers and followers gained). Evidence used on the
slide is his own LinkedIn data only: best post 19.22% engagement on a real scene; broad post 74,295
impressions at 0.21%. No targets or YouTube/Instagram benchmarks are claimed.
