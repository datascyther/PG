## BEAT 4 — the bay door (1:30–2:15) [R-06]

Every function upstream of this room believes the work is finished earlier than it is.

Sales believes it is finished when the order is confirmed. Planning believes it is finished
when the stock is allocated. Finance believes it is finished when the invoice is raised.

Nothing is finished until this door opens and a vehicle leaves with the goods on it. Outside
this room, a plan is a claim. Inside it, a plan is a physical constraint, and it either fits on
the vehicle or it does not.

That is the only honest test of an operating plan I have ever seen, and it happens on a floor
with a loading bay, not in a meeting room.

## BEAT 5 — the number (2:15–2:45) [R-06]

Four lakh is not a large number in a board meeting. It is a different number at this table.

At this end, a dispatch figure turns into a rate a person has to hold with their hands, for the
length of a shift, without dropping the pace in the last hour.

That is what a spreadsheet hides. Not the total, the pace. The total is a line item. The pace
is somebody's afternoon.

**ON SCREEN** — 4,00,000 · 29 Oct 2023 · his own post, that day.

and the other fifty-one are the only ones that compound.
