## 9. How the domains rotate

A channel dies when it becomes one domain in a loop. The batch rule, inherited from the
LinkedIn fortnight and carried into `05_CADENCE.md`:

> **Never two consecutive episodes from the same domain. Never two episodes in a row without
> a number in the title.**

That single constraint keeps a manufacturing-heavy slate from reading as an industrial
trade channel, which is the realistic failure mode of Option C.
