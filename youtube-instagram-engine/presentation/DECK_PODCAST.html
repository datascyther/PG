The idea

Long-form conversations (60-75 mins) with soldiers, intelligence professionals, military leaders, veterans, defence innovators, diplomats, and strategic thinkers to understand the human stories, decisions, sacrifices, and lessons behind India's national security ecosystem.

Why this idea?

Very few Indian podcasts are dedicated to this niche; Monks & Warriors is one. Raj Shamani, Prakhar Gupta and Ranveer Allahbadia cover it in parts, without a host from the sector. Puneet's years building defence technology give him the USP of drawing out the best insights from these guests.

Wider chair

The same chair also hosts founders, investors and AI builders who work on India's security and technology. No Indian show combines a defence insider with an operator who has built, raised and exited.

Why · growth and engagement

Why this plan grows the podcast

Discover
  • Clips on the dedicated handle; every guest shares the episode with their own audience
  • Measured by: clip views, reach, new viewers
Watch
  • The four-box arc: the person first, then suspense, so viewers stay through 60–75 minutes
  • Measured by: watch time, retention
Engage
  • The insights segment invites debate; viewers suggest the next guest in the comments
  • Measured by: comments, shares
Follow
  • Two episodes a month; five lanes bring founders, investors and AI viewers, not only defence
  • Measured by: subscribers gained

Guests are the growth engine: each one brings an audience the channel did not have. That is why the lanes widen beyond defence.
