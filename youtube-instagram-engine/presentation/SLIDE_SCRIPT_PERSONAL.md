# SPEAKER SCRIPT — @puneetgbuilds (personal YouTube + Instagram + Facebook)

For `presentation/DECK_PERSONAL.html` (18 slides). Open in a browser. Keys: arrows or click to move (some slides reveal step by step), `M` slide menu,
`N` speaker notes with a timer (these notes), `F` full screen, `?` help. Index rows are clickable. **About 14 minutes**, plus his questions. Direction only: execution comes after sign-off.

**Flow:** Content Strategy → Index → Ideas → What the ideas are → Strategic theme → Why (growth and
engagement).

**The one message to land:** five pillars, one for each part of his work, all built on one arc and
all aimed at depth of engagement, not just reach. One in three shorts on defence.

---

## 1 — Cover
**Say:** "This is the plan for your own channels, @puneetgbuilds, on YouTube and Instagram. Five
pillars, one for each part of your work. The podcast has its own plan."

## 2 — Vision
**Say:** "One sentence for both platforms. The first half is the edge, the second is the credibility."

## 3 — Brand Persona (a strategic choice)
**Say:** "This isn't a fact and it isn't the vision. It's how we want people to experience you, and every
word has to be earned by your record. Veteran: nineteen years building, eight in defence technology, an
industry veteran, not a military one. Credible: the 2019 National Entrepreneurship Award, the Series A,
an IIT Delhi-incubated company. Strategic: head of strategy at TAK, the jury seat, your own frameworks.
Approachable: plain language, the way you already write."
**If asked "why Veteran?":** it says depth of experience. The slide defines it so nobody reads it as
military service.

## 4 — Anti Positioning (guardrails)
**Say:** "These are guardrails, the three things we refuse to sound like, and each has a test. Not
preachy: show the decision and the number and let the viewer draw the lesson. No pro-politics narrative:
policy yes, party politics no. Not a typical motivator: no advice without your own example behind it."

## 5 — Two channels, two jobs
**Say:** "This channel carries your own view; the podcast carries other people's. Separate handles, so
each keeps its audience."

## 6 — Content Strategy
**Say:** "Now the strategy itself, for YouTube, Instagram and Facebook."

## 7 — Content Index
**Say:** "One track, five pillars, one per domain. Each month's shorts are made once and posted on YouTube, Instagram and Facebook. We never make separate content per platform. Then one strategic theme that every piece follows, and why it all grows the channel."

## 8–12 — The five pillars
Each slide reads the same way, top to bottom: **the idea → what the ideas are (YouTube, Instagram) →
strategic theme → why it drives growth and engagement.** Read the theme line, then the why line.

- **8 The Strategic Affairs (Defence):** theme: the decision behind the event. Why: defence moves with
  the news, so it is the main way new viewers find the channel; Versus and Weapon of the Week build a
  weekly habit.
- **9 Startup Breakdown (Entrepreneurship):** theme: one decision, one number. Why: breakdowns get
  saved and shared. **Ask him:** "What are you comfortable saying on camera about 2008–2016?"
- **10 The Investor's Table (Investor):** theme: both sides of the table. Why: questions invite
  comments and founders tag co-founders. **Ask him:** "What is the first question you ask every
  founder?" Write it down word for word.
- **11 Journey Decoded (Founder):** theme: the real decision, with the number and the cost. Why:
  people subscribe to a person, not a topic.
- **12 AI Decoded (AI):** theme: who owns it, what it costs, what it changes. Why: it pulls in an
  audience that would never follow a defence page, and explainers keep earning search views. Themes
  only, nothing about the current venture.

## 13 — Strategic Theme
**Say:** "Every short, post and carousel follows one arc: the moment, the context, your insider view,
the takeaway. That is what makes five pillars feel like one channel."

## 14 — Monthly Cadence & Mix
**Say:** "Twelve shorts a month, three a week, plus one post and one carousel. Every piece is made once
and uploaded to YouTube, Instagram and Facebook at the same time. Strategic Affairs goes out every week; the
other four pillars alternate in pairs. The carousel is a Startup Breakdown in week 2 and the post is a Journey
Decoded in week 3. Four of the twelve shorts, one in three, are defence. The podcast is not counted here."
**If asked about YouTube:** images and carousels go in the Posts tab (up to 10 images per post), not the Shorts shelf.

## 15 — Process
**Say:** "Four stages from strategy to monthly reports. Every fact checked against your public record,
nothing published without your yes."

## 16 — Why: growth and engagement
**Say:** "This is why it grows. Shorts, on all three platforms at once, bring people in; the arc keeps them watching; investor
questions and breakdowns get comments, saves and shares; five recognisable pillars turn viewers into
subscribers. Each stage has its own measure. Your own LinkedIn says the same thing: your highest
engagement-rate post of the year, 19.22%, was a real scene with a lesson, while a broad national-impact
post drew 74,295 impressions at 0.21%. We are aiming for depth, not reach."

## 17 — Three answers to move ahead
**Say:** "Three answers and we start on execution: is the vision right, are these the five pillars, and
is one short in three on defence the right balance?" **Then stop talking and write down his answers.**

## 18 — Appendix: the record behind every number
**Use only if asked "where does that number come from?"** Every figure on the slides is listed with its
source and status. Say plainly which are self-reported (his own public statements) and that ₹100 crore
ARR is company-reported (Inc42 reported FY24 revenue of ₹78 crore). Patents: 5 on his record; filings
were 15–20+. Stepping down: announced July 2025, role ended March 2025; the buyer is not public, never
name it. Defence years: TAK Technologies, head of strategy then core team building DarVi, not a founder
of TAK.

## Objections

**"Why not only defence?"** Defence stays the biggest pillar and the main way people find the channel.
The other four turn them into subscribers.

**"Will this look like I'm promoting my company?"** No. The AI venture is themes only; Clensta appears
only as a story.

**"What if I don't like a piece?"** One word kills it. Nothing publishes without his yes.
