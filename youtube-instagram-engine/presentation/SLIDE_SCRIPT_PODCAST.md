# SPEAKER SCRIPT — Podcast with Puneet

For `presentation/DECK_PODCAST.html` (16 slides). Open in a browser, `F` for fullscreen, arrow keys.
**About 12 minutes**, plus questions. Direction only.

**Flow:** Content Strategy → Index → The idea → What the episodes are → Strategic theme → Why (growth
and engagement).

**The one message to land:** a defence-led interview show whose chair also hosts founders, investors
and AI builders, on its own channels; every guest brings a new audience.

---

## 1 — Cover
**Say:** "The plan for the podcast. Its own YouTube channel and its own clips handle."

## 2 — Vision
**Say:** "The place where national security, founders, capital and AI are discussed by people who have
been inside them."

## 3 — Brand Persona (a strategic choice)
**Say:** "The same host, the same persona as your personal channel, each word backed by your record.
Veteran here means nineteen years in industry, eight in defence technology. With real veterans across
the table, the slide spells that out so nobody reads it as military service."

## 4 — Anti Positioning (guardrails)
**Say:** "Three guardrails for the show. The guest's story leads and the host doesn't lecture. Policy
yes, party politics no, and guests in or near public office are briefed on that before recording. No
hustle quotes: every insight is tied to a real decision the guest made."

## 5 — Content Strategy
**Say:** "Now the strategy for the show."

## 6 — Content Index
**Say:** "The idea, what the episodes are, who we invite, the theme every episode follows, clips and
cadence, and why it grows."

## 7 — The idea
**Say:** "Sixty to seventy-five minute conversations on the human stories and decisions behind India's
security. Very few Indian shows are dedicated to it, and none has a host who built defence technology, and the same chair also hosts
founders, investors and AI builders."

## 8 — What the episodes are: five guest lanes
**Say:** "Five lanes, two example episodes each. Defence is the core; the other four widen the
audience. Every guest is confirmed in writing before being named."

## 9 — Probable Guests
**Say:** "Six guest groups. Nobody is approached until you agree the list."
**Ask him:** "Which three would you want first?"

## 10 — Strategic theme (Podcast Theme)
**Say:** "Every episode follows one arc: the person, their professional life, the insights, the
future. Viewers know what to expect and still get surprised."

## 11 — Podcast Clips
**Say:** "Ten to twelve clips a month on a dedicated Instagram page, separate from your personal
handle."

## 12 — Monthly Cadence
**Say:** "Two episodes a month: one defence and strategy, one building, capital or AI."

## 13 — Process
**Say:** "Every fact checked, every guest confirmed in writing, nothing published without your yes."

## 14 — Why: growth and engagement
**Say:** "Clips and the guests' own audiences bring people in. The arc keeps them watching for an hour.
The insights segment starts debate in the comments. Five lanes turn defence viewers and founder viewers
into subscribers. Guests are the growth engine: each one brings an audience the channel didn't have."

## 15 — Three answers to move ahead
**Say:** "Three answers and we start guest outreach: your first three guests, one clips handle separate
from @puneetgbuilds, and one defence plus one wider episode a month?" **Then stop talking.**

## 16 — Appendix: guest check
**Use only if asked.** Lt Gen Hasnain is now Governor of Bihar and Dr Ajay Kumar is UPSC Chairman, so
both are off the list for now. Deepika Rathore is now Lt Col. Never pair Karnad and Sawhney. Dulat is
politically sensitive. FITT supported Clensta, so disclose that if Dr Nikhil Agarwal appears.
Bhuvneshwar Kumar is added because Puneet moderated his FITT session in Sep 2026, Puneet's highest
engagement-rate post of the year.
