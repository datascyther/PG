Why this plan grows the channel

Discover
  • Shorts on defence and AI, tied to the news, on all three platforms at once
  • Measured by: views, reach, new viewers
Watch
  • Every short built on the Moment → Takeaway arc, so viewers stay to the end
  • Measured by: average view duration, retention
Engage
  • Investor questions invite comments; breakdown shorts get saved and shared
  • Measured by: comments, saves, shares

  • Five recognisable pillars and weekly series; links across to the podcast
  • Measured by: subscribers and followers gained
