Podcast
with Puneet

Flagship
Long form · 60–75 min
Defence · founder
Investor · AI

The idea

Long-form conversations (60-75 mins) with soldiers, intelligence professionals, military leaders, veterans, defence innovators, diplomats, and strategic thinkers to understand the human stories, decisions, sacrifices, and lessons behind India's national security ecosystem.

Why this idea?

There's no dedicated podcast channel around this niche in India apart from Monk and Warriors. Likes of IRS Ravi Kapoor, Raj Shamani, Prakhar Gupta, Ranveer Allahabadia are doing in bits and pieces. Also they don't have that credibility of association with defence sector. Hence, Puneet has that USP of bringing out the best insights from the guests.

Beyond defence

The same chair also hosts founders, investors and AI builders who work on India's security and technology. No Indian show combines a defence insider with an operator who has built, raised and exited.

References

Untold Combat Stories ft. Col. Hemonto Panging Hav Liyakat Ali, SM — Monks & Warriors
