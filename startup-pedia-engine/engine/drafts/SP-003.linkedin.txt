Puneet Gupta has hired close to a thousand people, and counts the speed of hiring higher than the total

A photograph of a cabin, from the years when the company was small, reopened a question about hiring that Puneet Gupta had carried for a long time.

Over roughly a decade of building teams, he hired close to a thousand people. The counter he keeps is not on the total hired, but on how quickly a decision was made either way.

One early hire once told him he was too good to be true, a remark that did not sit well at the time and reads differently now.

The reflection published alongside the photograph was that what looked like empathy was sometimes hesitation.
