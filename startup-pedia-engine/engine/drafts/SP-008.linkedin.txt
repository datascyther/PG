Born Unicorn Season 1 put one line on the set wall: BOLD FOUNDERS BRIGHTER TOMORROW

Born Unicorn Season 1 was shot on 30 September and 1 October 2026, with a jury of six on the table.

The show was associated with AIC, Indian Institute of Technology, Delhi.

Behind the table, a panel on the set wall read BOLD FOUNDERS BRIGHTER TOMORROW, in white and yellow-green.

The backdrop carried a gold unicorn-head emblem inside a gold circle, with the show's name in gold capitals across it.

On 1 October 2026 a host in a black dress with a sequined bodice stood with four men on stage. An audience sat in front of them.

The crew worked into the early hours to get the last episode shot, sometimes until 2 AM. The season wrapped in October 2026.
