# Clensta's waterless product line has crossed one billion litres of water saved, the company said in April 2026

In April 2026, Clensta marked a cumulative figure reached with its waterless technology: one billion litres of water saved. [R-42]

The range rests on a small set of engineered technologies rather than a large catalogue. Five patents sit on the founder's public profile, covering rinse-free formulations, waterless technology and prolonged antimicrobial protection, with more than twenty patents filed in total. [R-08, R-28]

By 2021 the line was shipping roughly two lakh units a day, through more than 20,000 pharmacies and retail chains, with two crore litres of water saved to that point. By March 2024 the company reported ₹100 crore in annual revenue with 300% growth. [R-04, R-03, R-01]

Business Standard reported in July 2025 that the company had been acquired, at a last reported valuation of $36 million. [R-09, R-19]
