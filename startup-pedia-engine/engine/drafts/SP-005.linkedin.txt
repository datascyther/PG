Puneet Gupta's public record runs from an automation engineer in 2007 to nine dated roles by 2026

The record starts in 2007, as an automation engineer at Honeywell, and runs through nine dated roles to 2026.

He founded a consumer hardware company in 2016 and scaled it to ₹100 crore in annual revenue. He stepped down as chief executive in July 2025, after it was acquired at a last reported valuation of $36 million.

Across the same period he led more than 500 people, with over $100 million of enterprise value built and $30 million of capital raised.

The awards on his public profile run to twenty-five entries, including the National Entrepreneurship Award in 2019 and Times 40 Under 40 in 2022. He is partway through a Doctor of Business Administration at Golden Gate University, in Generative AI.
