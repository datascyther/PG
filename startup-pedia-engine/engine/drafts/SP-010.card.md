```
Investors don't bet on 50-page pitch decks. They bet on founders who execute through uncertainty.

Thirty founders, one room, two days. 5,000 to 6,000 applications had come in, and thirty were called to New Delhi to pitch.

I sat on that table. Six of us, a guest, and a crew still shooting after midnight on the last episode.

Here is the part nobody outside sees. A strong deck and a strong founder are rarely in the same person.

So what is an investor actually buying? Not the market size, and not the round.

It is how a founder behaves in the worst week the business has had. Nobody rehearses that, and no deck carries it.

Uncertainty is the only condition a startup lives in. The founder who keeps going through it is the asset, not the plan.

From two days on the Born Unicorn jury.
```
