A consumer hardware company that began in an IIT Delhi lab went on to run at ₹100 crore in annual revenue

In July 2026, at the Indian Institute of Technology, Delhi, Puneet Gupta traced the start of his last venture to technologies developed on that campus.

The company he co-founded in 2016 grew into a consumer hardware business with more than twenty patents, selling through more than 20,000 pharmacies and retail chains.

By March 2024 it reported ₹100 crore in annual revenue with 300% growth, and a stated target of ₹1,000 crore. Across the same period the founder led more than 500 people, with over $100 million of enterprise value built and $30 million of capital raised.

He stepped down as chief executive in July 2025, when the company was acquired, and now works from a board and advisory seat.
