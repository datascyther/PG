Delhi-based Clensta shipped four lakh products out of its warehouse on a single Sunday shift in October 2023

On a Sunday morning in October 2023, weeks ahead of the festive season, the entire Clensta team was in the warehouse, dedicated packers included. Around four lakh products went out that day.

The company had been shipping at that scale for a while. By 2021 it was moving two lakh units a day through more than 20,000 pharmacies and retail chains, with two crore litres of water saved to that point.

By March 2024 it reported ₹100 crore in annual revenue, with 300% growth and a stated target of ₹1,000 crore. The next year brought more than four times the growth of the year before, across a portfolio of more than thirty products.

Business Standard reported in July 2025 that the company had been acquired, at a last reported valuation of $36 million.
