# Most of a jury seat is writing, not talking.

On the second shooting day of Born Unicorn Season 1, most of my afternoon went by in one cream armchair. [N-11, R-89]

A pitch takes ninety seconds. The notebook stays open the whole time. I had a pen in my hand, and a phone face down on the table beside it. [N-10, R-92]

The hard part is not judging. It is keeping four things apart in one minute: the number, the claim, the promise, and the question you never asked. [R-78]

So what do you write when the applications are already down to thirty? [R-79]

You write the one question you would ask with another minute. That note is the only one that changes an answer. [N-10]
