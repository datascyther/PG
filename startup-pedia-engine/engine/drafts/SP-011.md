# Born Unicorn Season 1 put six jurors and a guest in a row of armchairs, and the table worked on paper

The episodes of Born Unicorn Season 1 were shot in New Delhi on 30 September and 1 October 2026. Six jurors sat at the table: Rahul Agarwal, Shammi Agarwal, Abhishek Goel, Vishal Nigam, Yogesh Thite and Puneet Gupta. [R-60]

Alok Pandey, CEO of AIC IIT Delhi, joined as guest jury. AIC IIT Delhi is the Atal Incubation Centre at the IIT Delhi Sonipat Campus in Haryana, and an ecosystem partner of the season. [R-61, R-85]

The table was a row of cream ribbed armchairs on a raised wood platform. Each chair had a side table on black hairpin legs, with a notebook, a pen, a water bottle and a vase of peach orchids. [R-91]

On the second shooting day one juror wrote in a notebook while the juror beside him spoke, pen raised. In another frame a juror wrote alone. [R-92, R-89]

The backdrop behind the chairs carried the show's name and SEASON 1 in gold capitals, under a gold unicorn-head emblem. [R-90]

The founders who came before that table worked in artificial intelligence, healthcare, consumer goods, mobility and sports. [R-62]
