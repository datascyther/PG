# Born Unicorn Season 1 was shot on 30 September and 1 October 2026, by a jury of six and a guest

Born Unicorn Season 1 was shot on 30 September and 1 October 2026. Six people sat on the jury table: Rahul Agarwal, Shammi Agarwal, Abhishek Goel, Vishal Nigam, Yogesh Thite and Puneet Gupta. [R-60]

Alok Pandey joined the table as guest jury. The panel mixed operators, investors and founders, including people from McKinsey & Company and Google. [R-61, R-67]

The cohort that pitched spanned five sectors: artificial intelligence, consumer goods, mobility, healthcare and sports. The show was associated with AIC, Indian Institute of Technology, Delhi. [R-62, R-64]

The crew worked into the early hours, sometimes until 2 AM, to get the last episode shot. [R-63]

Season 1 wrapped in October 2026, with more founders and more stories announced to follow. [R-66]
