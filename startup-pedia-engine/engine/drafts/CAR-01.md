# The quietest seat on the Born Unicorn set isn't on stage. It's the jury table, where a founder is heard by SIX PEOPLE.

On 1 October 2026, on the second shooting day, I sat at that table with a pen in my hand. [N-10, R-89]

Most founders on that floor have already run something real, and that is the easy part to see. The hard part is judging what I can test in a few minutes. [R-77]

So what do you test first when the field has already been cut to thirty? [R-79]

I start with their least certain number and ask them to prove it. [N-10]

A pitch that survives that question is worth the next hour. A pitch that only sounds good is not, and the table knows it within minutes. [N-10]
