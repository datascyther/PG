Born Unicorn Season 1 narrowed more than 5,000 applications to six founders, then took them to China

RASA Group, a New Delhi diversified conglomerate, launched the platform on 27 August 2026 under the name NEXT UNICORN – Season 1. It is built for founders who are already operating, not for ideas.

Applications ran from 17 August to 10 September 2026 and drew 5,000 to 6,000 entries from across India. Thirty founders were announced on 15 September, and presented their businesses in New Delhi on 25 and 26 September.

Six of them were selected. They travelled to China with RASA Group founder and CEO Rahul Agarwal for a business immersion at the Canton Fair in October. The trip was at zero cost to the selected founders, with flights, accommodation and itinerary hosted by RASA.

The six also become eligible for a potential investment of up to ₹10 crore from RASA Group, alongside strategic guidance and execution support.

The mentor's own record: more than 20 years building businesses, a former founder of Organic Harvest, and 100+ entrepreneurs mentored.
