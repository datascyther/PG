AIC IIT Delhi, the Atal Incubation Centre at the IIT Delhi Sonipat Campus, put its CEO on the Born Unicorn jury

AIC IIT Delhi was an ecosystem partner of Born Unicorn Season 1. The centre is the Atal Incubation Centre at the IIT Delhi Sonipat Campus in Sonipat, Haryana.

Its CEO, Alok Pandey, joined the jury as a guest, beside the season's six jurors.

The guest seat was not the only outside seat. The panel mixed operators, investors and founders, including people from McKinsey & Company and Google.

On the second shooting day a juror made a point with a pen raised in his right hand, a notebook open on his lap.

The platform's stated line was that India does not need more ideas, it needs more execution.
