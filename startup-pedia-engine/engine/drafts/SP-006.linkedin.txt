Born Unicorn Season 1 was shot on 30 September and 1 October 2026. Six people sat on the jury table: Rahul Agarwal, Shammi Agarwal, Abhishek Goel, Vishal Nigam, Yogesh Thite and Puneet Gupta.

Alok Pandey joined the table as guest jury. The panel mixed operators, investors and founders, including people from McKinsey & Company and Google.

The cohort that pitched spanned five sectors: artificial intelligence, consumer goods, mobility, healthcare and sports. The show was associated with AIC, Indian Institute of Technology, Delhi.

The crew worked into the early hours, sometimes until 2 AM, to get the last episode shot.

Season 1 wrapped in October 2026, with more founders and more stories announced to follow.
