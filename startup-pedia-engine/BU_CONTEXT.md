# BORN UNICORN — THE CONTEXT SHEET

This is the layer you write *on top of*. A Startup Pedia post is not a paragraph you
invent — it is a record: the platform, the funnel, the dates, the people, the sector, the
one number. All of that is already established here, with a source id for every line.

**How to use it:** every post about Born Unicorn is these fixed context lines plus one
founder and one number. The context lines carry the source id in brackets. When you write
a post, you copy the relevant context lines and fill the founder slot.

## 3. The founder-entry blueprint

This is the shape of a Startup Pedia founder entry. Four paragraphs, one number, one
attribution. The context lines are fixed; only the founder slot moves.

```
Headline   <City>-based <Company> <did what, with a number>

P1  THE WHAT AND WHEN
    <Company>, founded by <founder>, was among the 30 founders selected for Born Unicorn
    Season 1 from more than 6,000 applications. [R-xx]
    ← when the founder announced it themselves, attribute: "the company announced on
      <date> that it had been selected among the 30 finalists"

P2  THE WHAT THEY BUILD        ← the only paragraph that is really new
    <One or two sentences, plain, no adjectives. From the founder's own material.> [R-xx]

P3  THE CONTEXT
    Born Unicorn Season 1 was run by RASA Group, which took 5,000–6,000 applications down
    to a Top 30, then a two-day Founder Experience in New Delhi on 25–26 September 2026,
    then a Top 6 who travelled to Canton Fair in China, with an investment opportunity of
    up to ₹10 crore. [R-78, R-80, R-81, R-82]

P4  THE CLOSING
    The season's own line, on the set wall, was BOLD FOUNDERS BRIGHTER TOMORROW. [R-86]
    ← or a scale fact: "<Company> has <number>." — whichever is stronger
```

**The rules for the founder slot**

1. Name a founder only if **the founder announced it themselves, publicly.** Then cite
   their announcement. Never present it as our finding. [R-88 is the worked example]
2. One number, from the founder's own material. Not from a guess, not from a directory.
3. No adjectives where a number will do.
4. The image is one still, and it must be a still of the founder, the product or the set —
   not a group photo, not a logo, not a screenshot.

**Two things that post cannot do yet**, and both are on the ask list:
- **one number for the business.** The public announcement carries the selection, not the
  scale — revenue, chargers installed, cities covered. It has to come from the company's own
  material, and until it does, the post is a paragraph and not an entry.
- **a still of the founder.** The dump has set stills, not founder stills. A founder entry
  needs that founder's image, and it has to be one they have cleared.
