## SP-012 — Monday 23 November, 08:30

**Image:** `IMG_2619.jpg`
**Needs:** his word that the seat really was mostly writing

> **Most of a jury seat is writing, not talking.**
>
> On the second shooting day of Born Unicorn Season 1, most of my afternoon went by in one cream armchair.
>
> A pitch takes ninety seconds. The notebook stays open the whole time. I had a pen in my hand, and a phone face down on the table beside it.
>
> The hard part is not judging. It is keeping four things apart in one minute: the number, the claim, the promise, and the question you never asked.
>
> So what do you write when the applications are already down to thirty?
>
> You write the one question you would ask with another minute. That note is the only one that changes an answer.
>
> From one afternoon on the Born Unicorn jury table.
