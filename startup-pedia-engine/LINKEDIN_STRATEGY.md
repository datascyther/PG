## 1. Why this direction actually works for this profile

The archive says the shape that wins is *institution + year + named people + one
quotable line* (2026-07-22, 402 engagements, 13.19% ER) and *a real room he was in on a
real day* (2026-09-14, 19.22% ER — the best post of the year). It also says the trap is
the topic-driven post (2025-12-06: 74,295 impressions, 159 engagements, **0.21% ER**).

A pedia-format post is a documented, dated, sourced account of something that happened.
That is exactly the shape that wins, expressed in a register the account has never used
before — which is also why it will not read like the last thirty posts.

The Family N posts keep working as the personal layer. The Family P posts are the record
layer: the chronology of what was built, where it came from, who was in the room. Together
they give the profile something it does not currently have — a reason to exist as a
document, not only as a commentary feed.

## 2. Cadence

| Slot | Time | Content |

| Friday | 08:30 IST | Family P — a dated, sourced record |

- 3 posts a week. A skipped slot is fine; a weak post is not.
- 08:30 IST is a **house default, not a finding** — the native export has no post
  timestamps, so no time-of-day claim may ever be made.
- Thursday, Saturday and Sunday are weak on both measures. Do not schedule there.
- Batch of two weeks prepared in advance, approved in one 20-minute meeting, published
  on its slot. A batch is not a blanket approval: every post still needs its own yes.

## 4. One post, five surfaces

A single Family P post is not a LinkedIn post. It is the master copy.

| Surface | What it becomes | Effort |
|---|---|---|
| LinkedIn post | the post, as written | 0 |
| LinkedIn comment | reply to every substantive comment on his own posts within 24h, plus 3–5 comments a week on other people's posts | 15 min/day |
| X | the same post, then a 5–7 post thread built from the paragraphs in order | 10 min |
| Threads | the same copy, one paragraph per post, no link | 5 min |
| YouTube Short | only if a raw video exists for that moment — cut the 60s that carries the fact | 30 min |
| Reddit | **only if it is genuinely a question someone there is asking.** Never a link drop. Reddit punishes self-promotion harder than any other surface on this list | 20 min |
| Profile | one Featured slot per published post, on top of the 7 unused articles | 2 min |

The comment surface is the one that produces depth without producing another post, and
it needs nothing from the pipeline. It is the cheapest line item in the whole plan.

## 5. What is deliberately not done

- No repost-as-content. His one "big congratulations" post ran at 0.80% ER.
- No hashtag stacking. The hashtag era ended with the regime change on 2026-07-18:
  23 of 35 posts had hashtags before, 1 of 20 after.
- No link in the body. Only 3 of 50 top posts carried one, and those 3 median 0.98% ER
  against 7.24% for the 47 without. Link goes in the first comment, if anywhere.
- No video and no carousel in the body of a P post. 0 of 50 top posts are video or
  document. The format is text plus one image.
- No post built from a topic. Every post needs a moment.
- No impressions-based judgement. Reach and depth trade off: correlation −0.31 between
  impressions and engagement rate.

## 6. Governance

- Nothing publishes without an explicit yes. No silent tier, no auto-post, no
  "publishing on your behalf unless you object".
- Approval is batched in one 20-minute meeting every fortnight. His side of the meeting
  is three replies, not a review.
- His verbs: capture, answer, yes, no. One word kills a post, no penalty, no follow-up.
- Every published post is logged in `engine/publish_log.csv` with its impressions,
  engagements and ER pulled from the native export. The export caps at 50 posts and has
  no per-post history, so this log becomes more valuable than the export within three
  months.
- Facts come from the anchor bank. If a number is not in the bank with a source, it does
  not go in a post. The engine enforces this per paragraph; humans enforce it by not
  bypassing the engine.
